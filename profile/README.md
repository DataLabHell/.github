# Data Lab Hell

Data Lab Hell (DLH) is a research and technology organisation and as an R&D Incubator & Innovation Lab unique in its form. We are not profit-oriented, meaning we reinvest our profits entirely into R&D, and consistently pursue applied research and development as a cooperation partner.

Diverse data-based methods for industrial applications, decentralised and adaptive algorithms, as well as innovative mathematical, physical and algorithmic models require a highly interdisciplinary environment. This is reflected in our team: mathematicians, statisticians, computer scientists and physicists work together on new theoretical models, experimental platforms and innovative research approaches.

DLH is a unique space where data-driven innovation is not only conceived but ignites – we live "Igniting Data-Driven Innovation".
